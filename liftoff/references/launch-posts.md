# Launch posts

## Program rules

- Share each template in a **public X post that includes its share link**.
- Use X's **paid partnership label** on posts that promote your templates.

## Main post

```
[hook: the job it does or the time it saves. Under 110 chars]

[what it does, 1 line]

what you get:
→ [routine / output 1]
→ [routine / output 2]
→ [routine / output 3]

copy it free: [share link]
```

Write 2 hook options. Attach a screenshot or a 15-30s screen recording of the bot really working, because proof gets bookmarked.

## Hook angles that fit templates

- **The job:** "a grok bot that sends you every SpaceX launch in your timezone before you wake up"
- **The time saved:** "i stopped checking 6 sites every morning. this bot does it at 08:00"
- **The steal:** "copy my grok bot in one click. it [does X] every day"
- **The proof:** "this is what my bot sent me this morning, untouched:" plus a screenshot

## Follow-up quote-posts (days 2-7)

1. A real output from today
2. A user's question and the bot's answer
3. A changelog: "v2: added X because you asked"

## Rules

- First line under 110 characters and understandable without context.
- No em dashes. Use a period, a colon or →.
- No hype words (game-changer, unlock, revolutionary, insane, guaranteed, passive income).
- **Never invent** user counts, testimonials or earnings. A money figure needs a screenshot.
- Run `scripts/liftoff.py post FILE` before posting.
