# Grok Bot Creator Rewards: what we know

Compiled 2026-10-06 from public reporting and X's help/legal pages. The pilot is new and the rules may change, so check the official pages before you rely on anything here.

## The program

- **Name:** Grok Bot Template Rewards (pilot). Some coverage calls it Grok Bot Creator Rewards.
- **Launched:** late September 2026, announced around Sep 26.
- **Format:** invite-only pilot. Creators publish Grok Bot templates on X and may get discretionary payments based on usage.

## Eligibility

- Invited participant
- 18 or older
- Based in the United States, where X Money is supported
- Has a Grok Bot account
- Has an X account in good standing
- Has an eligible X Premium subscription or is affiliated with a Premium Business account

## How payouts work

- Rewards are decided **every two weeks**.
- They're paid in **USD** through X's regular creator payout run to your **X Money** balance.
- Factors named publicly:
  - **How many people use your templates**
  - **How often or how consistently they keep using them**
- Rewards are **discretionary**. They're not a revenue share and there's no guaranteed income.
- The program is separate from Original Content Rewards.

## Posting rules

- Share each template in a **public X post that includes its share link**.
- Use X's **paid partnership label** on posts promoting your templates.

## How templates work

- Shipped **Aug 28, 2026**.
- In bot settings, **Share as template** creates an anonymized private copy. You then choose to share it with your team or create a public link.
- Anyone with the link gets an **independent copy** in their own account, like a recipe rather than the meal.
- **Travels:** identity and description, skills, routines, relevant memories, and many first-party plugins.
- **Doesn't travel:** conversation history, the author's computer, logins, custom code and private MCP servers.
- **Secrets aren't stripped automatically.** Anything in the description, skills or routines ships unless you remove it.
